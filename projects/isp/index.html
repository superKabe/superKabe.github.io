Image Signal Processor

What is happening

Six steps, each with a specific job.

  1. DemosaicEstimate the missing color channels at every pixel.
  2. Color and gammaApply the project’s color matrix and display curve.
  3. DenoiseUse an edge-aware filter to reduce small variations.
  4. SharpenRecover local detail after the denoising pass.
  5. ContrastMap the useful intensity range to the final output.
