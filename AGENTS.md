# Portfolio content and design guidance

- Keep project pages engineering-first and concise. Lead with the method, constraints, implementation, and result.
- Avoid oversized hook statements, generic marketing language, and dramatic copy that does not add technical information.
- Prefer compact technical notes over repeated narrative sections.
- Write in Kaden's direct first-person voice. Keep claims specific and grounded in the repository, résumé, or supplied context.
- Do not use em dashes in site copy.
